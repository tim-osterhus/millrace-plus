# LAD provenance

This workflow adapts the twelve role prompts, transition policy, contracts and
templates from Tim Osterhus's LAD Factory at commit
`58e87f7d8941de144e29cdd619b41cff2a78b770`, under Apache-2.0. The unchanged
full license is `licenses/lad-factory-Apache-2.0.txt`. `provenance.json` preserves
all 44 original selected inputs, hashes, URLs, destinations and exclusions.

The remediation also consulted seven corrected semantic inputs at commit
`a7e5a19a319786fb3138fb938764c5d77607b7b0`: contracts, transitions, routing,
nested-model guidance, Planner, Checker and the Claude Code adapter README.
Their exact audited hashes and adaptation destinations are recorded separately
under `corrected_reference`; the original attribution is not relabelled.
`adaptation.destination_hashes` records the current shipped workflow assets,
including the unchanged license and native profile, excluding these two
self-describing provenance files.

The adaptation uses editable graph-v4 copies and session-v2 occurrence history,
explicit invalid-result recovery, independent requirement-side review, immutable
parent baseline evidence, selective guidance and original-run Learning seams.
It removes LAD quotas while retaining finite stage deadlines, exact owners,
writer controls and explicit user limits. Native helper schemas have offline
validator fixtures only; no live helper facility is qualified. Explicit retained
session upgrade needs matching public runtime controls and a separately owned
maintenance window. Actual used-copy operations remain unqualified here.

Factory Orchestrator, Goal, chat, heartbeat, runtime and host configuration are
not installed. The original AGENTS snapshot remains inert attribution evidence,
not package authority. No NOTICE or selected-file copyright headers existed in
the pinned original input; none are invented. Linked ICM/history/runtime/paper
material remains attribution context only: its code, templates, text and figures
are not imported, nor are their separate licenses represented as this package's
license. This adaptation does not claim canonical ICM conformance or transfer
native harness qualification.


Shared-target support adds a validated service claim and immutable target record
to fresh child bootstrap and prepared assignments. It preserves the twelve role
prompts, standalone target contract, immutable baseline backup and Learning seam.
Destination hashes cover the changed worker and contract files. Original and
corrected-reference input lists, upstream commits and license remain unchanged.

The v0.23.2 routing follow-up (L-R1) consulted Factory `context/transitions.md`,
`context/contracts.md` and `prompts/learning.md` at commit
`e4d587290ceeaa886c6486ae03bd9a15831cf691`, compared from the corrected reference.
`provenance.json` records their exact hashes under `routing_followup_reference`.
They are semantic inputs only. Millrace adopts a finite deterministic
presentation view and one nonrecursive Learning assessment per packet; L-R1
does not adopt Factory's prose interpretation, Orchestrator or provider waiting.
The later L-R2 provider-wait routes (`scripts/provider_wait.py`,
`context/provider-policy.json` and the generated provider nodes) are
Millrace-authored; they adopt no Factory provider-waiting text or Orchestrator.

The v0.23.2 engineering feedback packet (L-Q1) adds Millrace-authored opt-in
engineering capture, deterministic check phases/guards and requirement history
(`scripts/engineering_feedback.py`, `context/engineering-profile.example.json`
and edits to the role contracts, selected prompts and existing scripts). It uses
no new upstream input. User-supplied benchmark conversations motivated the checks
but are not imported or claimed as evidence of improvement.
